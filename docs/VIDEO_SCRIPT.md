# Gyaan Setu: demo video script

Length: about 6 minutes. Each scene lists what to show on screen and what to
say. Record on a laptop in Edge or Chrome, using the build from
`npm run build` and `npm run preview` (http://localhost:4173) or the
deployed site once it is updated.

**Before recording**
- Open the app once and ask one Telugu and one Hindi question in Ask Gyaan,
  so the AI model and translator are already downloaded. The first download
  takes a few minutes and should not be in the video.
- Create a demo student account. Answer one quiz level with two or three wrong
  answers, so the Practice page has something to show.
- Turn the computer's sound on for the read-aloud scene.

---

## Scene 1: Opening (0:00 to 0:30)

**Screen:** Home screen with the sunrise banner.

**Say:**
"Hello! This is Gyaan Setu, which means 'a bridge of knowledge'. It is a
learning app for school students from Class 1 to Class 10, in English,
Telugu and Hindi. Its biggest strength is that it works without the
internet. Once it is opened, the lessons, quizzes and even the AI assistant
keep working offline, on the student's own device."

---

## Scene 2: Language and account (0:30 to 1:00)

**Screen:** Choose a language (English, తెలుగు, हिन्दी). Log in with the demo
account. Point to the "Offline Ready" status.

**Say:**
"The student first chooses a language: English, Telugu or Hindi. The whole
app changes to that language. Each student has a simple offline account with
a name and a 4-digit PIN, so progress is saved on the device, even with no
internet."

---

## Scene 3: Classes and lessons (1:00 to 1:45)

**Screen:** Pick a class (for example Class 9). Show the list of 10 chapters.
Open one lesson: picture, explanation, key points, activity.

**Say:**
"Every class now has at least ten chapters. That is 106 lessons in total,
from 'My Body' in Class 1 to 'Electricity' and 'Heredity' in Class 10. Each
lesson has a picture, a simple explanation, key points and an activity the
student can try at home, all in the three languages."

---

## Scene 4: Quizzes with levels (1:45 to 2:45)

**Screen:** Start the quiz. Show the Level 1 and Level 2 tabs, with Level 2
locked. Choose answers, and point out that nothing is marked yet. Press
Submit.

**Say:**
"Every lesson has a quiz of ten questions, split into levels of five. Level 2
stays locked until Level 1 is passed. To pass, the student needs at least
three correct answers out of five."

**Screen:** After Submit: green for correct, red for wrong, and the
explanation under each question. The result card says "Level 1 passed!" and
Level 2 unlocks.

**Say:**
"The student answers all five questions first and then presses Submit. Only
then does the app show which answers were right and which were wrong, with an
explanation for every question. This way students think for themselves first
and then learn from their mistakes. Passing a level opens the next one, and
passing the last level completes the lesson and earns stars."

---

## Scene 5: Practice (2:45 to 3:20)

**Screen:** Open Practice. Show "Quizzes to try again" with a Retry button,
and "Questions you got wrong". Answer one and press "Check answer".

**Say:**
"The Practice page shows exactly where the student needs help. First, the
quizzes where they scored low, with a button to try that level again. Then
every question they answered wrongly, to practise once more with the
explanation. If the student has done well everywhere, the page simply says
there is nothing to practise."

---

## Scene 6: Dark mode (3:20 to 3:35)

**Screen:** Tap the moon button in the top bar, then tap it again.

**Say:**
"There is also a light and dark theme. One tap switches it, and the app
remembers the choice. That's easier on the eyes when studying at night."

---

## Scene 7: Ask Gyaan, the AI assistant (3:35 to 5:15)

**Screen:** Open Ask Gyaan. Show the English, తెలుగు and हिन्दी buttons at
the top.

**Say:**
"Now my favourite part: Ask Gyaan, the AI assistant. It runs fully on the
device, with no internet and no server, so the student's questions stay
private."

**Screen:** In English, ask "Why do we need sleep?" and show the answer.

**Say:**
"In English, the AI can answer almost any question a student asks."

**Screen:** Switch to తెలుగు. Type "ohms law" in English and send. Show the
Telugu answer.

**Say:**
"Now I switch the chat to Telugu and type 'ohms law' in English. The answer
comes back in Telugu, because the answer is always in the language selected
in the chat. Here it uses the Class 10 lesson's own Telugu text."

**Screen:** Still in Telugu, type a question in Telugu, for example
"సూర్యుడు ఎందుకు వేడిగా ఉంటాడు?", and show the answer.

**Say:**
"Questions can also be typed in Telugu. Behind the scenes, Gyaan translates
the question into English, the AI answers it, and the answer is translated
back into Telugu. All of this happens on the device, using a translation
model built for Indian languages."

**Screen:** Switch to हिन्दी. Ask "What is a black hole?" and show the Hindi
answer. Tap the 🔊 button to play it aloud. Then tap the mic and speak a
question.

**Say:**
"The same works in Hindi. Every answer can be read aloud with the 🔊 button,
in English, Telugu or Hindi, and students can also ask by speaking into the
microphone. That helps younger children and those who are still learning to
read."

---

## Scene 8: Offline (5:15 to 5:40)

**Screen:** Turn off Wi-Fi (or set the browser offline). Reload. The app still
opens, shows "Offline Ready", and a lesson still opens.

**Say:**
"Here is the internet switched off. The app still opens, the lessons and
quizzes work, and progress is saved. This is what makes Gyaan Setu useful for
students in villages and places with weak or costly internet."

---

## Scene 9: Closing (5:40 to 6:00)

**Screen:** Home screen.

**Say:**
"So, that's Gyaan Setu: lessons from Class 1 to 10, quizzes with levels and
explanations, a Practice page for weak areas, a dark mode, and an AI
assistant that answers in English, Telugu and Hindi by text and by voice,
all working offline. Thank you for watching!"

## Short version (about 1 minute)

"This is Gyaan Setu, an offline learning app for Classes 1 to 10 in English,
Telugu and Hindi. Every class has at least ten chapters, with pictures,
explanations and activities. Quizzes come in levels of five questions: the
student answers, submits, and then sees the correct answers with
explanations, and passing a level unlocks the next. The Practice page brings
back the quizzes and questions they found hard. Ask Gyaan, the AI assistant,
answers any question in the language chosen in the chat. Type 'ohms law' in
the Telugu chat and the answer comes in Telugu, and it can read answers
aloud and listen to spoken questions. And it all works without the internet."

## Notes for the presenter

- If asked how the Telugu and Hindi AI works: "The AI model thinks in
  English. A translation model made for Indian languages (IndicTrans2 by
  AI4Bharat) translates the question in and the answer out, on the device."
- If asked about size: "The first time, the AI downloads about 270 MB, and
  Telugu or Hindi chat adds about 300 to 570 MB. After that it works offline."
- Be honest if asked about accuracy: the assistant is small, so answers are
  usually good but not always perfect. Lesson answers are hand-written.
